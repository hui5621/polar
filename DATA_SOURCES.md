# Environmental Data Acknowledgments

The fixed environment maps combine sea-ice, ocean-current, bathymetry, and chart-related information.

- Ocean-current fields: Copernicus Marine, **Arctic Ocean Physics Analysis and Forecast**, product `ARCTIC_ANALYSISFORECAST_PHY_002_001`, DOI [10.48670/moi-00001](https://doi.org/10.48670/moi-00001).
- Bathymetry: **GEBCO_2025 Grid**. See [GEBCO terms of use](https://www.gebco.net/data-products/gridded-bathymetry/terms-of-use).
- Sea-ice chart classes: U.S. National Ice Center Arctic Marginal Ice Zone products. See the [USNIC product catalog](https://usicecenter.gov/Catalog).

These maps are intended for offline research. GEBCO grids are not intended for navigation or other safety-at-sea purposes.
